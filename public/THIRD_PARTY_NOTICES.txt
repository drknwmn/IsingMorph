# Third-party software

GIF encoding uses [gifenc](https://github.com/mattdesl/gifenc), version 1.0.3,
by Matt DesLauriers and contributors, under the MIT license. Its upstream
license and notices are retained by the installed package. Vite bundles its
code into the local browser worker; no runtime CDN request is needed.

The example circle, star, and heart are generated by this application's canvas
code. The neuron, brain, and person PNGs are bundled from the original Ising
Morph project. Their source filenames, unchanged-file hashes, and metadata
limits are recorded in docs/example-assets.md in the source repository.
No runtime fonts or datasets are downloaded from third parties.
