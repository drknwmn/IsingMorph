Behind the fluctuations

A field of tiny choices.
A figure finding its form.

Each pixel is a spin in a two-dimensional Ising lattice. Neighboring spins prefer to agree; temperature lets them change. Your image becomes a spatial field that gently guides those choices.

In Dissolve & reform, that guidance fades between images. In Direct morph, the guiding field shifts from one image to the next. Try silhouettes, bold diagrams, or high-contrast figures.

This is an image-guided visualization using Metropolis dynamics and periodic boundaries. The zero-field critical temperature is a reference for the model, not evidence that an image transition is a thermodynamic phase transition. Simulation steps are not physical time.
